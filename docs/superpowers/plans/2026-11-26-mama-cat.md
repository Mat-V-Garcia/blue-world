# Mama Cat Boss Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Replace the third boss with a grown "Mama Cat": a new rigged model and her own cat-style fight (stalk, swipe, pounce, flop, tail play, fishing).

**Architecture:** A new rules file `src/mamaCat.js` exposes the same outside shape as `createBoss`/`createFalcon`, so `main.js` drives it unchanged apart from picking it for `kind: 'cat'`. A new `src/mamaCatModel.js` loads the Magnific GLB and poses it per state. `src/boss.js` goes back to being the tarantula's alone.

**Tech Stack:** three.js 0.170, Vite, `node --test` (tests in `tests/*.test.mjs`), Magnific (image, then Image to 3D with rig).

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-26-mama-cat-design.md`

## Global Constraints

- She is about 6.5 units long; the lizard is 1.6 and jumps about 1.3 high.
- Six hits; a hit is landing on her back during `flop` or `play` only.
- No shots of any kind. Push-ups do not scare her.
- Flop time is the same all fight (2.0 s). Sleepy (below half health) adds a yawn and a double pounce.
- She is never hurt and never scary: beaten = asleep where she is, still visible.
- Name in all player-facing text: "Mama Cat".
- Magnific work happens in the Personal project. Ask Mathew before each download.
- No randomness in `mamaCat.js` rules (tests step it frame by frame at 1/60 s).

## Review Focus

- **Lizard hides while she is crouched to pounce:** she must not pounce through the table; she cancels to `stalk` and then fishes.
- **Tail dropped while she is in the air, flopped or shaking off:** `baitWith` returns false and nothing changes.
- **Pounce aimed past the fence or onto furniture:** the landing spot is clamped inside the yard and she still flops there.
- **Lizard still on her back when the flop ends:** it is bounced off, not hurt.
- **Level restart mid-move (`reset()` during `pounce`, `play` or `fish`):** she is hidden again at full health with the shadow ring hidden.
